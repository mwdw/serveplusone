Serving has never been easier

2026 may go down as something of a high water mark (for now) for serve dominance in men's tennis:

- The hold rates for the top 50 men on hard courts are unprecedented at 85%. In 2006 a top 50 player was broken once every 5.1 service games on hard courts, in 2026 it’s once every 6.7
- Tour average serve speeds are seeing local highs over the last couple of years (120mph avg 1st serve speed)...
- ...yet 1st serve in % for the top 50 is also at an all time high in 2026 (64%)
- double faults are close to record lows at 3%

This year, 42 of the current top 50 have held 80%+ of service games on hard courts (82%), a record since 1991. In 2006 it was fewer than half.

40% of tour 1st serves that land in never come back on hard courts (this is even higher for the top 20 at 42% and higher still for the top 10 at 43%). On hard courts 1 in 4 points now ends on an unreturned 1st serve, and 1 in 3.6 when a top 10 player is serving
