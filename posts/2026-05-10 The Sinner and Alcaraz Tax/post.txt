The Sinner & Alcaraz Tax

How top-20 servers fare when the next ball comes back through Sinner or Alcaraz.

A side-by-side flow chart comparing first-serve outcomes against the field and against Sinner or Alcaraz.

Data: 2025–2026, all surfaces
Alt: Two flow charts of first-serve points for top-20 servers in 2025–26: against each other (72.4% won) and against Sinner or Alcaraz (64.8% won). Against the pair, fewer first serves go unreturned (32% against 38%), and the server's +1 shot wins less often on both the forehand (6.5 points lower) and the backhand (8.4 points lower).
