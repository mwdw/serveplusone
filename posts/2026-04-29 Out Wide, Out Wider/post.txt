Out Wide, Out Wider

Lefty servers really do seem to have a special something on slice wide serves.

Returners on average make contact 33cm wider on a lefty wide serve on the ad side than on a righty wide serve on the deuce side. Some of this is down to return positioning, forehand vs backhand return mechanics etc.

Data: Top 10, 2025–2026 first-serve returns on wide serves, excluding aces
Alt: Court diagram of where top-10 players make return contact on wide first serves in 2025–26, split by service box and by the server's hand. On the ad side they make contact 2.63m outside the singles line against left-handers and 1.60m against right-handers; on the deuce side it is 1.34m against left-handers and 2.30m against right-handers.
